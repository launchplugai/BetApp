# Sherlock Audit Engine Roadmap

**Status:** Implementation plan (Phase 3)  
**Prerequisites:** Dashboard groundwork complete (related to Slice 2A/2B)  
**Goal:** Build an anti-narrative engine to validate claims for betting context  
**Date:** 2026-02-12

---

## Overview

**Sherlock's job:** Prevent narratives from turning into misinformed betting decisions.

**Sherlock does NOT determine bets.**  
Instead, it validates narrative claims to ensure they are based on sound evidence and not intuition.

---

## Core Functionality

### Audit Workflow
1. **Receive Claim:** Sherlock audits claims before they affect picks.
   - Example claim: "This player is chasing numbers."
2. **Gather Evidence:** Check the data layer (DNA signals) to find supporting evidence.
3. **Find Counterevidence:** Look for contradictory signals (data that challenges the claim's validity).
4. **Quantify Confidence:** Determine how confident we are in the claim's accuracy based on evidence.
5. **Assess Actionability:** Finally, evaluate whether the claim should change the betting decision or strategy.

**Output Structure:**
```json
{
  "claim": "This player is chasing numbers",
  "support": [
    "Usage up 15% in last 5 games",
    "Historical increase when near contract expiration"
  ],
  "counter": [
    "Team is actively competing for playoffs",
    "Recent performance suggests stabilization"
  ],
  "confidence": 0.82,
  "failureModes": [
    "Player's usage could stabilize due to passing from stars",
    "Coach sentiment could motivate fatigue management"
  ],
  "recommendation": "Consider player over, but monitor minutes"
}
```

---

## Audit Variables

### Required Inputs
- **Claim details:** What’s the claim?
- **Supporting data:** What signals support it?
- **Counter signals:** What indicates the claim could be wrong?
- **Historical context:** What can we learn from previous games or similar situations?
- **Confidence measurement:** How confident in this claim based on gathered data?
- **Actionability check:** Does the claim alter the betting strategy?

### Outputs
- **Audit summary:** Clear conclusion on whether the claim can be trusted. 
- **Recommended actions:** Suggested bets or cautionary notes based on the analysis.

---

## Function Structure

### Core Functions
1. **Audit Claims:** Analyzes every narrative claim that changes bets.
   - Input: Claim, context
   - Output: Audit summary

2. **Generate Evidence Reports:** Pulls evidence supporting a narrative claim.
   - Input: Claim
   - Output: Summary of supporting and counterevidence

3. **Confidence Calculation:** Measures the confidence level of a claim's support.
   - Input: Evidence and counterevidence
   - Output: Confidence score (0-1)

4. **Action Advice:** Decides on the implications of a validated claim.
   - Input: Claim summary
   - Output: Recommended actions for betting
   
---

## Integration Workflow

**Step 1:** Claims enter the system when a betting decision model runs.  
**Step 2:** Sherlock audits every claim output against the data generated by DNA.  
**Step 3:** The audit returns a verdict on claim validity, completes with supporting evidence and actionable recommendations.
**Step 4:** The engine generates new betting picks or modifies the existing strategy based on the output from Sherlock.
