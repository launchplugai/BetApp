## What Shipped

Built the Heuristic Engine - a signal detection layer that analyzes GameContext objects and returns structured heuristic signals. Two heuristics operational:

**pace_shock:** Detects when teams play at significantly different tempos. A 5+ possession difference scores 0.7-0.95 (high volatility). Example: Lakers (102.3 pace) vs Celtics (98.1 pace) = 0.72 score, high impact.

**rest_asymmetry:** Detects rest advantages. 3+ days difference = high impact. Gives the rested team an edge detection signal.

Both heuristics return: `{name, score, explanation, volatility_impact}` for DNA/Sherlock to consume.
